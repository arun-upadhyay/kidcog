# Question bank fixes — 3 October 2026

An automated read-through of all 1,811 questions in `server/questions/` flagged 414 with a real problem. Every flagged question was rewritten in place (same id, age and category, so history and "seen" tracking still work), and 26 new questions were added to fill gaps. Nothing was disabled. All questions remain `reviewed: false`.

## What was wrong

Issues found (a question can have more than one): wrong or debatable key 201, ambiguous 73, off category 54, missing visual 34, broken text 26, factual error 13, age inappropriate 13.

The worst kinds were prompts that point at a picture that isn't there ("look at these two sandwiches" with no sandwiches, two identical 🐸🐸 to compare), answer keys that were wrong or a matter of taste (Christmas as the "best" holiday, taking a lost puppy home, closing a window to cool a hot room), a literal `\n` shown in nine age-4 prompts, and a few factual slips (balloons, spelling patterns).

## How they were fixed

Picture references were removed: questions now describe things in words, or use emoji that clearly differ (🍎 vs 🍌), never size, shade or two identical emoji. Wrong keys were corrected so exactly one option is clearly right for factual questions. Taste and opinion questions either give full marks to every reasonable choice or were turned into open questions that reward giving a reason (53 questions changed type). Safety answers (tell a grown-up, wear a helmet) always score highest. Perfectionism questions reward calm, flexible responses to mistakes.

New questions: 20 `strong_ideas` questions for age 5 (that age had none, so it was falling back to the database bank) and 6 `observant` questions for age 4 (word and emoji puzzles that need no picture).

Separately, 20 prompts beginning "Look at these…" / "You see these…" whose items are given in words were reworded to "Here are some…" so nothing implies a picture.

Every changed file passes the same checks as `questionFiles.ts` (uuid id, age, trait, 3 options a/b/c with the answer key scoring 3, 4-level rubric) plus: no picture wording and no `\n` in prompts. MCQs are scored by `optionScores`, so options sharing the top score all count as correct.

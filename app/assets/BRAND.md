## Cog, the KidCog mascot
- Cog is a friendly, bright-orange gear with a cream face, big eyes, rosy cheeks and a yellow "thinking spark" on top: the "cog" in KidCog.
- Cog was designed for KidCog in October 2026. It is drawn directly as SVG shapes (`app/src/components/Cog.tsx`, `app/assets/icon-source.svg`), was not traced from or based on any existing character, logo or stock artwork, and uses no third-party image assets or fonts.
- Colours: gear `#FF9F1C`, rim `#FFB547`, face `#FFF4DC`, outline/mouth `#3D2C8D`, spark `#FFD23F` (edge `#F2A900`), cheeks `#FF8FA3`; app purple `#6B4BB0`.
- App icons (`icon.png`, `adaptive-icon.png`, `favicon.png`) are rendered from the same SVG. Regenerate them from `icon-source.svg` after any change to Cog.
